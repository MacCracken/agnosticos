# garjan

Environmental and nature sound synthesis (Sanskrit गर्जन — *roar / thunder*) — weather, impacts, surfaces, fluids and fire from physical models, with no samples.

- **Repository**: [github.com/MacCracken/garjan](https://github.com/MacCracken/garjan)

- **Status**: Stable — v1.0+
- **Language**: Cyrius
