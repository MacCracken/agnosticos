## What It Does

- **Culture**: First-class entity with identity, geographic roots, diaspora, time span, status (active/dormant/revived/historical/extinct), core values, practices, art forms, key figures, sacred texts, material culture, subcultures, and language associations.
- **CultureRelation**: Relationships between cultures — influence, syncretism, branching, conflict, exchange. Temporal and directional.
- **Practice**: Rituals, ceremonies, daily practices, festivals, art practices, martial practices. Categorized with frequency and sacred/secular distinction.
- **ArtForm**: Music, dance, visual art, literature, oral tradition, theater, architecture, textile, culinary. Key works and figures per form within a culture.
- **Lineage**: Parent/child culture relationships and subculture trees. "What influenced this culture?" / "What did this culture produce?"
- **Query**: By region, time period, status, relationship type. "Which cultures coexisted in the Mediterranean in 300 BCE?" / "What art forms does hip-hop culture practice?"

## Relationship to Itihas

Itihas owns WHEN events happened — civilizations, eras, dates, calendars. Hadara owns the cultural CONTEXT events happened within. A war in itihas has a cultural backdrop in hadara. Events are temporal; culture is the fabric those events are woven into.

## Relationship to Sangha

Sangha models social STRUCTURES — class, kinship, institutions, group dynamics. Hadara models cultural IDENTITY — values, practices, art, transmission, lineage. A society has structure (sangha) and culture (hadara). One is the skeleton, the other is the living tissue.

## Relationship to Avatara

Avatara provides mythological ARCHETYPES — divine personality templates. Hadara provides the cultural CONTEXT that produced them. Thoth is an avatara; ancient Egyptian culture is a hadara. The archetype belongs to the culture; the culture gives the archetype meaning.

## Relationship to Bhava

Bhava CONSUMES cultural display rules from hadara. Emotional expression varies by culture — intensity, restraint, directness, formality. Hadara provides the cultural rules; bhava applies them to personality modeling.
